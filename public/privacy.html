Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Pinnacle Edge Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates Media Engine, an automated image and document generation platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use the Service:

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Payment information: when you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed directly by our payment processor, Paystack. We do not store your card details ourselves.
  • Usage data: the templates you create, the images you generate, and basic usage logs (timestamps, request counts) needed to operate and bill the Service.
  • Technical data: standard server logs such as IP address and browser type, collected automatically for security and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To send essential account and billing communications (e.g. signup confirmation, payment receipts)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To operate Media Engine, we rely on the following trusted service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database hosting and user authentication
  • Paystack — payment processing
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (e.g. signup confirmations)
  • Render — application hosting

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing how they handle data.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information and generated content for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal or accounting purposes.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Cookies

We use only the minimum technical cookies/session tokens necessary to keep you logged in securely. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made.
