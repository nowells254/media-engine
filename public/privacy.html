Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Media Engine collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Media Engine is operated by Pinnacle Edge ("we", "us").

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, country, email address and password. The password is stored in a protected form by our login provider and we cannot read it.
  • Content you provide: the text, logos, photos, signatures and designs you enter or upload, and the images we generate for you.
  • Payment information: card and M-PESA payments are processed by Paystack and we do not receive your card details. For Alipay we keep the payment code, the amount and the note you type. We keep a record of your passes, orders and payments.
  • Certificate records: for each certificate, its ID, the recipient's name, the course or title, the issuer's name and the date.
  • Technical data: your login token and language choice are saved in your browser, and our servers keep ordinary logs.

2. How we use it

We use it to run your account, create the images you ask for, take payments, show your history, verify certificates, prevent misuse, give support and meet legal duties. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the service: Supabase (database, login and file storage), Render (hosting), Paystack (payments) and our email provider. They may use it only to provide their service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. Certificate verification is public

Anyone who has a certificate's ID or QR code can see the recipient's name, the course or title, the issuer and the date for that certificate. Only create certificates for people who know about it.

5. Files and links

Images you upload or generate are kept in cloud storage and are opened through long, hard-to-guess links. Anyone who has a link can open that file, so share links carefully. Only upload photos of people with their permission.

6. Where it is stored

Our providers use servers outside Kenya and China, for example in Europe and the United States. By using the service you understand that your information may be processed there.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and content until you ask us to delete them or you close your account. Payment records may be kept for as long as the law requires. Certificate records are kept so that verification keeps working, unless you ask us to remove them.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by contacting us. You can change your name, phone number and country in Settings at any time. You may also have rights under the data protection law that applies to you, including the Kenya Data Protection Act.

9. Security

We use secure connections, protected passwords and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

10. Children

The service is for people aged 18 and over.

11. Cookies and similar storage

We store your login and language choice in your browser so the site works. We do not use advertising cookies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

13. Contact

To use your rights or ask a question, email nowells254@gmail.com.

隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 10 月 16 日

本政策说明 Media Engine 收集哪些个人信息、为什么收集,以及您的选择。Media Engine 由 Pinnacle Edge(“我们”)运营。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 账号信息:您的姓名、手机号码、国家/地区、邮箱和密码。密码由我们的登录服务商以加密形式保存,我们无法读取。
  • 您提供的内容:您输入或上传的文字、标志、照片、签名和设计,以及我们为您生成的图片。
  • 付款信息:银行卡和 M-PESA 付款由 Paystack 处理,我们不会收到您的银行卡信息。对于支付宝付款,我们会保存付款编号、金额和您填写的备注。我们会保存您的套餐、订单和付款记录。
  • 证书记录:每张证书的编号、获得者姓名、课程或标题、颁发方名称和日期。
  • 技术数据:您的登录凭证和语言选择保存在您的浏览器中,我们的服务器会保存常规日志。

2. 我们如何使用

我们用于运营您的账号、生成您需要的图片、收款、显示您的历史记录、验证证书、防止滥用、提供支持以及履行法律义务。我们不会出售您的信息,也不会将其用于广告。

3. 我们与谁共享

仅与帮助我们运营服务的服务商共享:Supabase(数据库、登录和文件存储)、Render(托管)、Paystack(支付)以及我们的邮件服务商。它们只能为向我们提供服务而使用这些信息。法律要求时,我们也可能披露信息。

4. 证书验证是公开的

任何持有证书编号或二维码的人,都可以看到该证书的获得者姓名、课程或标题、颁发方和日期。请仅为知情的人制作证书。

5. 文件与链接

您上传或生成的图片保存在云存储中,通过很长且难以猜测的链接访问。任何拥有链接的人都可以打开该文件,因此请谨慎分享链接。上传他人照片前,请先取得本人许可。

6. 存储位置

我们的服务商使用位于肯尼亚和中国境外的服务器,例如欧洲和美国。使用本服务即表示您理解您的信息可能在这些地方被处理。

7. 保存期限

在您要求删除或关闭账号之前,我们会保存您的账号和内容。付款记录可能按法律要求的期限保存。证书记录会保留,以便验证功能持续有效,除非您要求我们删除。

8. 您的权利

您可以联系我们,要求查看、更正或删除您的个人信息,或对我们的使用方式提出异议。您可以随时在“设置”中修改姓名、手机号码和国家/地区。依据适用于您的数据保护法律(包括肯尼亚《数据保护法》),您可能还享有其他权利。

9. 安全

我们使用安全连接、加密保存的密码和访问控制。没有任何系统是绝对安全的,请使用强密码并妥善保管。

10. 未成年人

本服务面向 18 周岁及以上的用户。

11. Cookie 与类似存储

我们在您的浏览器中保存登录状态和语言选择,以使网站正常运行。我们不使用广告 Cookie。

12. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策,顶部的日期显示最新版本。

13. 联系方式

如需行使您的权利或有任何疑问,请发送邮件至 nowells254@gmail.com。
