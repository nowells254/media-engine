Support Center

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us

How does Media Engine work? ▾
Choose a template (Product Banner, Certificate, Event Poster, or Quote Card), enter your title, subtitle, and optional image, then click Generate. Your image is created in seconds and ready to download.
What's included in the Starter plan? ▾
$49/month includes 1,000 generations, access to all template types, and high-resolution downloads. If you go over 1,000 in a month, you can purchase additional generations without losing access.
Can I create my own custom templates? ▾
Yes — in the Templates tab of your dashboard, you can create and save as many templates as you like based on our starting designs, and reuse them anytime.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▾
Contact us using the email below and we'll process your cancellation. Self-service cancellation is coming soon.
Is my payment information secure? ▾
Yes. All payments are processed directly by Paystack, a licensed payment processor. Media Engine never sees or stores your card details.
Can I use generated images for commercial purposes? ▾
Yes, images you generate using your own account are yours to use, including commercially, as described in our Terms of Service.

Still need help?

Send us an email and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
