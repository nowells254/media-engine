Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

How does Media Engine work?

Open Studio, choose a product, pick a style and colours, add your details, then press Generate. Designing and previewing are free. You pay when you generate, either per image or with a pass.

What do the passes include?

There are Daily, Weekly and Monthly passes. Each includes a set number of images for its period. A pass starts when your payment is confirmed and does not renew automatically. Current prices are on the Plans & Billing tab and the home page.

Can I pay per image instead of buying a pass?

Yes. At the last step in Studio, choose pay per image. You see the total before you pay, and your images are made as soon as the payment is confirmed.

How can I pay?

Cards and M-PESA are paid in Kenyan shillings through Paystack. If you choose Chinese Yuan, you pay by scanning our Alipay QR code, writing the payment code in the remark box and pressing "I have paid". We check these payments by hand, which can take some hours.

How do I make certificates or cards for many people?

In Studio step 3, fill in the first person, then press "+ Add another" and change the details. You can also import a CSV file in step 4. Everything is made together at the end.

What are the certificate ID and QR code?

Every certificate gets its own ID and QR code. Scanning the code opens a page that shows who the certificate was issued to, for what, by whom and when. It only confirms the certificate was created on Media Engine, not any accreditation.

How do I add a signature?

In Studio step 3 press "Draw signature" and sign with your mouse or finger, or upload a photo of a signature on white paper. You can save a default signature in the Brand tab.

How do I change the language?

Use the language button at the top right of the page. The whole site switches between English and Chinese.

My payment went through but nothing happened

Your payment is safe. Open the Studio and press "Try again" if it shows an error. If it still does not work, email us with the time of the payment and your payment code.

How do I change my email or password, or delete my account?

Change your email, password and personal details in Settings and Profile. To delete your account and data, email us from your account email address.

Contact us

Email nowells254@gmail.com. Please include your account email and, for payments, the payment code.

支持

常见问题解答,以及如何联系我们。

Media Engine 如何使用?

打开“设计室”,选择产品,选择样式和颜色,填写您的信息,然后点击生成。设计和预览免费,生成时才付费,可按张付费或购买套餐。

套餐包含什么?

我们提供日卡、周卡和月卡,每种套餐在有效期内包含一定数量的图片。套餐在付款确认后生效,不会自动续费。当前价格见“套餐与账单”页面和首页。

可以不买套餐,按张付费吗?

可以。在设计室最后一步选择按张付费,付款前会显示总价,付款确认后立即为您生成图片。

如何付款?

银行卡和 M-PESA 通过 Paystack 以肯尼亚先令付款。如果选择人民币,请扫描我们的支付宝二维码付款,在备注栏填写付款编号,然后点击“我已付款”。我们会人工核对这些付款,可能需要数小时。

如何为很多人批量制作证书或名片?

在设计室第 3 步先填写第一个人的信息,然后点击“+ 再添加一个”并修改信息。您也可以在第 4 步导入 CSV 文件。最后会一次性全部生成。

证书编号和二维码是什么?

每张证书都有自己的编号和二维码。扫描二维码会打开验证页面,显示证书颁发给谁、事由、颁发方和日期。它只确认证书是在 Media Engine 上创建的,并不证明任何认证。

如何添加签名?

在设计室第 3 步点击“手写签名”,用鼠标或手指签名,或上传白纸上签名的照片。您也可以在“品牌”页面保存默认签名。

如何切换语言?

使用页面右上角的语言按钮,整个网站会在英文和中文之间切换。

我已付款但没有任何反应

您的付款是安全的。如果设计室显示错误,请点击“重试”。如果仍然无法解决,请发邮件告诉我们付款时间和您的付款编号。

如何修改邮箱、密码或删除账号?

您可以在“设置”和“个人资料”中修改邮箱、密码和个人信息。如需删除账号和数据,请使用您的账号邮箱给我们发邮件。
